# UI review: bulk-sell

Verdict: **APPROVED** (no blocking defect; 5 non-blocking findings to polish).

Reviewed on 2026-10-31 against `spec.md` and the layout table of `design.md`, commits 611cb30..586a50a. Method: dev server (`npm run dev`, port 5174) with the e2e fake backend (`game/e2e/support/fakeBackend.js`) driven by a Playwright script kept outside the repo; 17 pieces (3 worn, 14 in the bag, all five rarities). Screens: Equipamento at 1280x720 and 390x844 in the states: mode off (`Vender várias`), mode on with 0 selected (submit disabled), 3 selected plus a worn piece clicked (locked, no effect), tooltip hover in selection mode, rarity button `Raro`, confirmation, success notice. The fake backend has no `POST /equipment/sell` route, so the sale answer was stubbed in the script (the notice text comes from the stub: 5 pieces, 777 copper). Console: no 404, no errors (only the WebGL GPU-stall driver message). No horizontal overflow at either width.

## Matches spec
Yes. `Vender várias` sits at the right of `Mochila (n)`; hidden in selection mode; tiles get the accent outline, lift and round check badge; worn pieces are dimmed and not selectable; the bar shows `Selecionadas: k · Total: ...`, five rarity pills (Comum, Incomum, Raro, Épico, Lendário), `Vender selecionadas` (disabled at 0) and `Cancelar`; the confirmation reads `Vender 5 peças por 17 prata 50 cobre?` with `Sim, vender` / `Não` and replaces the actions; the success notice `5 peças vendidas por ...` shows in the green `.equip-notice` pill. Singular and plural forms are handled. All text is Portuguese. Palette, heading font, pill buttons and tile frames match the rest of the Equipamento screen.

## Findings (most severe first)

1. **Low-Medium - Equipamento, selection mode, desktop 1280x720 - the bar pushes the bag out of view.** The bar is about 112 px tall (about 140 px with the confirmation), so with 14 pieces only the first row of tiles is visible and the second row is cut by the window edge; the player has to scroll the window to see the tiles they are selecting. Should: the tiles stay visible next to the bar. Fix: make `.bulk-sell-bar` `position: sticky; top: 0` inside the scrolling window, or put the summary, rarity pills and the two actions on one or two compact lines (summary and actions on the same row). File: `game/public/style.css` (`.bulk-sell-bar`, around line 2052).
2. **Low - rarity pills, all states - the 4 px left border renders as a curved sliver, and the pressed state is too faint.** Because the pill is fully rounded, the coloured border follows the curve and reads as a thin crescent, not the stripe in the design; `Comum` (`#c0b6a5`) is almost invisible on the bar. The pressed pill (`aria-pressed="true"`, `#dcd3c4` on `#ebddc5`) differs from its neighbours only slightly, so it is hard to tell which rarities are on. Fix: use a small coloured dot or a `box-shadow: inset 4px 0 0 <colour>` with a smaller radius, and make the pressed state stronger (rarity-coloured fill at about 25% or a 2 px border in the rarity colour, plus bold). File: `game/public/style.css` (`.bulk-sell-rarity*`, lines 2076-2106).
3. **Low - phone 390x844 - the whole bar is tiny.** The game canvas and DOM are scaled down as a whole on phone width (pre-existing for every screen), so the 13 px pills and 15 px summary render at about 5-6 px and the tiles at about 24 px: hard to read and to tap. Not caused by bulk-sell, but this feature adds the first many-small-targets control. Suggestion for a later task: raise the DOM scale on narrow viewports. Not blocking.
4. **Low - tooltip in selection mode.** Hovering a tile shows the piece tooltip above it, and for the first bag row it covers the rarity pills and the actions (screenshot with `Raro Amuleto +1` over the bar). Fix: hide `.piece-tooltip` while `sellMode` is on, or place it below the tile. File: `game/src/adapters/phaser/views/equipmentView.js` or the tooltip CSS.
5. **Low - layout shift when entering selection mode.** The `Transferir conjunto` buttons disappear from the hero rows, so the hero blocks get about 20 px shorter and the whole window moves up as soon as `Vender várias` is clicked, which moves the button the player just pressed. Fix: keep the row height fixed (visibility hidden instead of removing the buttons, or `min-height` on `.hero-block`). File: `game/src/adapters/phaser/views/equipmentView.js` and `style.css`.

## Suggestions (not blocking)
- While confirming, every tile is disabled and fades, so the selected outlines and the check badges turn pale; keep the selected tiles at full opacity so the player sees what the question refers to.
- The 3 px selected outline at 6 px offset touches the outline of a selected neighbour (tile gap is about 8 px); they merge into one blob when several tiles in a row are selected. A 4 px offset would keep them apart.
- After the last piece is sold, the empty-bag text `Nenhum equipamento ainda. Vença chefes para encontrar peças.` is correct, but the window collapses to a small box; fine.
