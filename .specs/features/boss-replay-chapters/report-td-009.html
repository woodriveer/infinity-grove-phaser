Cenários e2e da correção

Arquivo game/e2e/bossReplayChapters.spec.js: 5 cenários novos, todos passaram na execução de cobertura (o arquivo inteiro rodou nela):

  • closing during a chapter load and reopening leaves the chips enabled on the current chapter
  • a chapter answer that arrives after closing and reopening does not change the shown chapter
  • a chapter load that fails after Esc closed Rever chefes shows no error on the reopened map
  • a new chapter load started before the late answer of a closed one arrives keeps the chips disabled until it answers
  • the game reopening Rever chefes after the last daily win keeps its map when a chapter load was pending
